# RADAR — AI-Powered Opportunity Intelligence

### Discover opportunities. Connect information. Enable better decisions.

Radar is an early-stage Brazilian technology project exploring how artificial intelligence can help small businesses discover and understand public-sector business opportunities.

## Our Mission

Make access to business opportunities more accessible to small companies through intelligent information discovery and organization.

## Our Vision

Build an intelligent, modular platform capable of identifying relevant opportunities and helping businesses make better-informed decisions.

## Core Areas

- Opportunity discovery
- Intelligent information analysis
- Public-sector opportunity monitoring
- Small business accessibility
- AI-powered workflows

## Current Development Stage

Early development and validation.

We are exploring the architecture, evaluating use cases, and identifying opportunities for collaboration.

## Looking for

- International collaborators
- AI developers and researchers
- Technical mentors
- Pilot partners
- Innovation communities

## Project Principles

- Transparency
- Reliability
- Evidence-based development
- Responsible AI
- Accessibility
